ZORA REIGN / BROOKLYN, NEW YORK
THE WOMAN BEHIND THE FREQUENCY

A little earth.
A little electric.

“I’m not here to sound easy to place.
I’m here to sound like myself.”

Zora Reign makes music for the spaces between strength and tenderness. Brooklyn shaped her ear: soul records in the living room, bass through the walls, verses written somewhere between the last train and the first light.

Her sound moves from velvet harmonies to verses with something to say. Her style follows the same rule: take up space, tell the truth, and leave a little room for mystery.

Concrete Honey is her first full-length statement. No costume. No borrowed confidence. Just roots, rhythm, and a woman who knows what she came to do.
