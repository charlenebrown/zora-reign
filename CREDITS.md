## Artwork prompt
Use case: ads-marketing. Premium original music artist campaign photograph, square, no text. Fictional Black woman neo-soul and hip-hop recording artist in her early 30s, deep brown skin, self-assured direct gaze, voluminous sculptural natural afro with deliberate braids, sculptural silver hoop earrings and silver rings, black structured outfit with burnt orange and acid green accents. Expressive funky fashion and soulful confidence. No resemblance to a real artist. Waist-up editorial portrait, dark espresso studio backdrop, warm orange rim light and emerald side light, analog film texture. No gold, text, logos, or watermark.
